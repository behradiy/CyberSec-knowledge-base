# Honey Pots

The `Honeypots` folder provides resources for understanding, deploying, and utilizing honey pots as a security measure. Honey pots are decoy systems designed to lure and capture attackers, providing valuable insights into their tactics and techniques.

**Content**

* Deployment guides - Step-by-step instructions for setting up different types of honey pots.
* Configuration examples - Sample configurations for popular honey pot software.
* Analysis techniques - Methods for extracting and analyzing data collected from honey pots.
* Additional resources - Links to relevant articles, documentation, and tools.

**Articles (read in order)**

* [01-what are them](01-what%20are%20them.md) - Honeypot basics: types, spam traps and limitations.
* [02-Intelligentones](02-Intelligentones.md) - Intelligent honeypots and the challenges they face.
* [03-integration with AI](03-integration%20with%20AI.md) - Why and how honeypots and AI work together.
* [04-LLM-powered honeypots](04-LLM-powered%20honeypots.md) - *New in the 2026 update:* honeypots that use large language models to improvise a fake system (Galah, Beelzebub, T-Pot, DECEIVE, Cowrie's LLM mode), and what the first real benchmark says about them.
* [05-Deception against AI attackers](05-Deception%20against%20AI%20attackers.md) - *New in the 2026 update:* catching AI hacking agents, prompt injection as a defense, canary tokens for MCP and AI agents, and the deception arms race.
