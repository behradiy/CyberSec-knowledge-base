# AI in Attack and Defense

The `AI-Attack-and-Defense` folder covers the two sides of the same coin: AI systems that find and fix software vulnerabilities, and attackers who use AI in real operations. Between 2024 and 2026 both went from research demos to everyday reality, and anyone working in cybersecurity now needs to understand them.

**Content**

* [01-AI bug hunters](01-AI%20bug%20hunters.md) - From Google's Big Sleep and the DARPA AI Cyber Challenge to frontier models finding thousands of zero-days, plus the benchmarks that track progress and the problems this created (AI slop, patching bottlenecks).
* [02-How attackers use AI](02-How%20attackers%20use%20AI.md) - What threat-intelligence teams actually observed: from phishing helpers to malware that queries LLMs, AI-orchestrated espionage and the first AI-built exploits in the wild, with a myth-busting section and detection ideas.

**Reading Tips**

Most of the big numbers in this area come from the companies that build the models. Each article says when a claim is a vendor's own and links to independent evaluations where they exist. If you only read one thing, read the "Catch" and "Myth-Busting" sections: they explain why headlines and reality differ.

Related folders: [Securing-AI](../Securing-AI/) (protecting AI systems themselves), [Honeypots](../Honeypots/) (trapping AI attackers) and [IDS](../IDS/) (AI in detection and the SOC).
